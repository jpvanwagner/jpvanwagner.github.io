Licensed CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Resized to 256 px and bundled as text. Where each one is used:
  WoodFloor051 https://ambientcg.com/view?id=WoodFloor051 light oak plank floor: Reference, Program Room, entry
  Carpet007    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet007    polka-dot carpet: Children's Section
  Carpet016    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet016    beige carpet: Study Rooms
  Carpet012    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet012    blue-grey commercial carpet: staff offices
  Carpet006    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet006    carpet tiles: PC Lab
  Tiles036     https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles036     white ceramic tile: restrooms, break room
  Terrazzo005  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Terrazzo005  terrazzo: entryway and hall
  Concrete036  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete036  stained concrete: basement
  Concrete034  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034  smooth concrete: sidewalks, block walls
  Road012A     https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Road012A     asphalt: parking lot
  Grass004     https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004     lawn
  Bricks101    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bricks101    red brick: outside walls
  Plaster001   https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001   plaster: inside walls
The teen zone's arcade carpet and the shelving are Mapster's own drawn textures.

PATRON FIGURES (js/patron-models.js)
------------------------------------
"Mini Characters 1" by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters):
ten characters, glasses, a cane and a wheelchair, with their animations.
Licensed CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain); the pack's license file is
assets/kenney-mini-characters-License.txt. Repacked as GLB (texture embedded,
unused animations removed) and bundled as text.
glTF loading: three.js GLTFLoader and SkeletonUtils r128 (mapster/lib/),
MIT License, same as three.js below.
