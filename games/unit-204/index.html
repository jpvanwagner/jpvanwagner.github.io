      SOP 4.2 · Move-Out Condition Codes
      0 · Clean / No Action

      Functional, sanitary and as expected. Nothing to do.

      1 · Normal Wear & Tear

      The slow, unavoidable decline that comes from living somewhere: faded or matted carpet in walking paths, small nail or anchor holes (¼″ or less), worn finishes, aged caulk, rubber parts that wear out. Never charged to the deposit.

      2 · Tenant Damage

      Harm caused by accident, neglect or abuse: stains, burns, holes bigger than ¼″, broken glass or fixtures, drawing on walls. Charged to the deposit, and only if it's documented with a photo.

      3 · Maintenance Hazard

      Anything that threatens safety or the building: active leaks, gas odor, electrical heat or sparking, disabled smoke alarms, mold, fixtures that could fall. File an emergency work order and photograph it. A hazard is coded 3 even if a tenant caused it.

      Field method
      • Look at a feature to bring up a close-up. Then touch, smell and use tools on its parts.
      • Measure holes and stains. Size often decides between wear and damage.
      • Smell catches what eyes miss: gas, urine, mildew, burning.
      • Use the clipboard on a feature to mark it. Attach a photo to every damage or hazard finding.
