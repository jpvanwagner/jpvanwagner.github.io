      Unit 204

      Game design, writing, code, and (some) art by Joe VanWagner.

      Sound effects

      Kenney (kenney.nl). Footsteps, clicks, chime, knock, ratchet, and city ambience, released under CC0. They come from Kenney's open-source starter kits:

      • Starter Kit 3D Platformer: footsteps, chime, and knock
      • Starter Kit City Builder: clicks, tick, swish, and city ambience
      • Starter Kit FPS: ratchet

      The camera shutter, pencil, tape measure, phone ring, and alarm beeps are synthesized live in the browser.

      Music

      Inspired by various free tracks on Pixabay, but synthesized by Joe VanWagner using the ZzFXM tracker.

      Nine original songs written for the game as ZzFXM tracker files (they're in the game's music folder, ready to open in the ZzFXM tracker), played with ZzFXM by Keith Clark and Frank Force and ZzFX by Frank Force, both MIT License.

      The tenant phone calls were built in Scenemaker, a branching-scenario tool.

      Icons

      • Look, Touch, Hammer, and Camera: recolored from Streamline's free Pixel icons, CC BY 4.0
      • Talk and Clipboard: recolored from pixelarticons by Gerrit Halfmann, MIT License
      • Walk, Smell, Tape measure, Flashlight, Screwdriver, and Pencil: drawn for this game

      Fonts

      • Jersey 15 by Sarah Cadigan-Fried (source), SIL Open Font License
      • Silkscreen by Jason Kottke (source), SIL Open Font License
