A.I. Use Policy and Ethics

Clear, practical rules for using A.I. tools at work: what is allowed, how to keep information safe, and why ethics matter.

I built this course to roll out an A.I. use policy to everyone in an organization, from officers and managers to contractors. It turns a dense policy into plain guidance: approved tools, what data may never go into a prompt, how to check A.I. output, and how to report a concern.

Audience: All employees, managers, contractors, and consultants who use or manage A.I. tools.
