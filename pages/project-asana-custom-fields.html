Mastering Custom Fields for Asana Transition Projects

Software training that shows transition and recruiting teams exactly which Asana fields to update, when, and by whom.

I built this course so project teams could keep dozens of site transitions consistent in Asana. It explains each portfolio- and task-level custom field, walks through real examples of updating them, and shows leaders how to read the resulting portfolio metrics.

Audience: Transition, recruiting, and operations teams, plus the leaders who review their portfolios.
