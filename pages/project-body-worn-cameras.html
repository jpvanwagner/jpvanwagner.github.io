Daily Duties & Body-Worn Camera Expectations

Prework for in-person body-worn camera training for retail asset protection officers.

I built this course as prework for Asset Protection Officers (APOs) at retail sites in the Bronx, so they arrived at in-person training already knowing their post orders, daily duties, and how and when to use their body-worn cameras (BWCs).

Audience: Retail asset protection officers, ahead of hands-on BWC training.
