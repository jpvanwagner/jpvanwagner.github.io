C-TPAT Import Container Security Training

Required knowledge for security professionals at distribution centers and supply chain sites.

I built this course to train the security professionals I worked with at logistics and supply chain sites on the Customs Trade Partnership Against Terrorism (C-TPAT): what it is, why it matters to clients, and exactly how officers inspect, verify, and report on import containers.

Audience: Security officers and site leadership at distribution centers.
