Curriculum

Collecting and connecting material to learners and expectations.

Texas: high school English

As a High School teacher in Texas, I have developed, refined, and customized curriculum materials to create cohesive roadmaps and outlines for diverse instructional teams across multiple subject levels.

As a cadre, select members of our department collaborated to ensure that all relevant Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS; standards) were properly assessed both formatively and summatively. Using adopted curriculum materials including Springboard, Patterns of Power, and other supporting content, we identified what students would learn, how it would be taught, and in what ways our students would be able to demonstrate the skills and knowledge gained. While our cadre as a whole developed quarterly, semester, and year-long structures, individuals and teams within the cadre differentiated assignments, assessments, and projects to address expectations for students in both Core- and Advanced-level classes to develop rigorous and engaging units of study.

Alaska: middle school, cross-curricular

As a Middle School teacher in Alaska, I developed, taught, and shared cross-curricular lessons supporting and embracing Iñupiaq values, culture, history, language, and worldview as a foundation for instructional support in all content areas. District transdisciplinary teams created a variety of units combining skill expectations from multiple core subject areas while collaborating with community stakeholders including families and Elders. Community members lent their knowledge and expertise to help ensure that lessons would integrate cultural values within the Iñupiaq Learning Framework.

My adaptability and expertise in navigating evolving professional landscapes have consistently enabled me to deliver high-quality and effective educational resources that cater to varying needs and ensure success.
