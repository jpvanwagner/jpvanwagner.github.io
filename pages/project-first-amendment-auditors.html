Professional Engagement with First Amendment Auditors: Hands-Off Security Compliance

How security officers stay calm, lawful, and hands-off when someone films them to provoke a reaction.

I built this course after a rise in "First Amendment audits" at client sites. It explains what auditors want, where public and private space begin and end, and gives officers a simple script for professional, de-escalating engagement that protects them, the client, and the company’s reputation.

Audience: Security officers and site supervisors at public-facing sites.
