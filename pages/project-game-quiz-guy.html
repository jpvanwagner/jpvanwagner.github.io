Quiz-Guy

A maze-chase quiz, built to turn software training review into something people want to replay.

A maze-chase game: eat the dots, dodge the ghosts, and eat a glowing ? block to get a quick review question. Answer it right to power up and turn the tables on the ghosts. Three mazes (Library, City Blocks, and Rings), one per level; play as Quiz-Guy or Quiz-Gal.

This demo: easy general trivia (geography, science, animals, history, and more). In training, it reviewed Asana basics.
Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, or click anywhere in the maze and Quiz-Guy finds his own way there. Answer questions with the mouse or number keys. On a phone or tablet, tap the maze or use the D-pad.

Play full screen

Tip: click inside the game first so it can hear your keyboard. On a small screen, try the full-screen version.

Make your own quiz game!

Type in your own multiple-choice questions and get a copy of Quiz-Guy that asks them instead. Save it to your desktop and double-click to play, send it to your students or team, or put it on your own website. Each question can have 2 to 4 answer choices; click the circle next to the right one.

Want to change more than the questions? The Quizcade Builder edits everything in Quiz-Guy and its sister games: the on-screen words, rules, colors, sprites, and backgrounds and mazes. You can paste questions in from a spreadsheet, watch a live preview, and copy paste-ready HTML for a web page, an embed, or a Rise 360 Code block.
