Quiz-Man

A maze-chase quiz, built to turn software training review into something people want to replay.

A maze-chase game where eating power pellets triggers a quick review question. Play as Quiz-Man or Ms. Quiz.

This demo: easy general trivia (geography, science, animals, history, and more). In training, it reviewed Asana basics.
Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move. Answer questions with the mouse or number keys.

Play full screen

Tip: click inside the game first so it can hear your keyboard. On a small screen, try the full-screen version.

Make your own quiz game!

Type in your own multiple-choice questions and get a copy of Quiz-Man that asks them instead. Save it to your desktop and double-click to play, send it to your students or team, or put it on your own website. Each question can have 2 to 4 answer choices; click the circle next to the right one.
