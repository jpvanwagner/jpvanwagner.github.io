Quizaga

A space-shooter quiz, built to turn software training review into something people want to replay.

A fixed-shooter space game. Four crab-shaped ships carry the letters A to D: shoot the one holding the right answer to the question above the playfield, while dodging the ships that dive at you.

This demo: easy general trivia (geography, science, animals, history, and more). In training, it reviewed workforce-management reports and dashboards.
Controls: A/D or arrow keys to move and Space to fire, or drag with the mouse and click to fire. On a phone or tablet, use the D-pad and the FIRE button.

Play full screen

Tip: click inside the game first so it can detect your keyboard. On a small screen, try the full-screen version.

Make your own quiz game!

Type in your own multiple-choice questions and get a copy of Quizaga that asks them instead. Save it to your desktop and double-click to play, send it to your students or team, or put it on your own website. Each question can have 2 to 4 answer choices; click the circle next to the right one.

Want to change more than the questions? The Quizcade Builder edits everything in Quizaga and its sister games: the on-screen words, rules, colors, sprites, and backgrounds. You can paste questions in from a spreadsheet, watch a live preview, and copy paste-ready HTML for a web page, an embed, or a Rise 360 Code block.
