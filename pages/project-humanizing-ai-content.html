Humanizing AI-Generated Content

Spot the telltale signs of AI-generated writing, then edit it into something that sounds like a person wrote it.

AI can draft polished text in seconds, but on its own it smooths away the voice, detail, and insight that make training stick. I built this short course for the people who write our training and communications: how to recognize the patterns AI leaves behind, practical edits that put the human back in, and habits that keep your own writing standing out.

Audience: Anyone who writes or edits training, communications, or other content with help from AI tools.
