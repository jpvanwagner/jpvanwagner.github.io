Mindfulness & Wellness

Promoting physical, social, and mental health.

Calm Schools

The Calm Schools initiative was a program designed by Calm.com to bring mindfulness and meditation practices to schools. I used its variety of resources to guide group meditation with the goal of promoting emotional well-being, reducing stress, and enhancing focus and concentration.

I also piloted Rhithm (2020–22), a social-emotional learning app with quick student check-ins that help counselors and teachers spot who needs support.

Restorative justice practices

During the challenging years following the initial COVID-19 lockdowns, our district adopted a philosophical approach to addressing harm and conflict that emphasizes healing, accountability, and repairing relationships. One of the key activities to aid in this process was "Circles," where participants come together in a structured dialogue to engage in open communication, active listening, and collective problem-solving. During these sessions, I was able to create a safe space for individuals to share their experiences, feelings, and perspectives, fostering empathy, understanding, and ultimately, seeking resolution and restoration.

Hope Squad Trusted Educator

In partnership with the Grant Halliburton Foundation, I help to identify at-risk youth and to connect them with campus and community resources. I was among the first on our faculty to be elected a "Trusted Educator" to provide students the support they need.

Gay-Straight Alliance liaison

I am proud to have served as the campus liaison for the student Gay-Straight Alliance (GSA), creating a safe and inclusive space for students to express themselves and support one another. Through guidance and advocacy, the GSA successfully organized various events promoting LGBTQ+ acceptance, including guest speaker promotion, awareness campaigns, and collaborations with community organizations. The increased participation and positive feedback from students, as well as the growing membership of the GSA, serve as evidence of the success achieved in fostering an inclusive and supportive environment on campus.

Health & Social Services partnerships

To promote mental health in schools across Alaska's North Slope, Health and Social Services provides resources, training, and support to school staff and students by conducting mental health screenings and offering counseling services. As a teacher, I played a vital role in identifying students who needed support, connecting them and family members to appropriate services, and creating a nurturing and inclusive environment supporting students' overall well-being.

We additionally collaborated with North Slope communities to promote overall health by implementing comprehensive drug abuse education programs, conducting community outreach initiatives, and providing resources for prevention, intervention, and treatment. This partnership aimed to raise awareness, empower community members, and create a supportive network to address drug abuse and promote healthier lifestyles across the region.
