Commercial Property Security Standards (Jacksonville, Florida)

Post orders for officers at a commercial office property: duties and conduct, access control, property handling, documentation, and emergencies.

I built this course to replace a long post-orders document with something officers actually finish. It covers the two security roles on site (lobby officer and security desk), professional conduct and customer service, visitor and access control, property removal, lost and found, and keys, incident documentation (with a video walkthrough), communication tools, and emergency and escalation procedures, ending with a quiz.

Audience: Security officers and supervisors assigned to a commercial office property.
