Retail Security Standards Training

Post orders for officers at a national retail chain: guest service, professional image, patrol tours, and emergencies.

I built this course to prepare security officers for a retail client whose stores put guest experience first. It covers the uniform and conduct standards, shift and confidentiality procedures, completing patrol tours in a mobile app (with a screen-recorded walkthrough), and what to do in an emergency.

Audience: Security officers assigned to retail stores, and their supervisors.
