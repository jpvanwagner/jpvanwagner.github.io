Supervisors as Trainers: Building Officer Readiness

Prework for a train-the-trainer program for security supervisors.

Supervisors are the link between written Post Orders and officer performance. This course prepares security managers and supervisors to conduct effective on-the-job training for their direct reports, as prework for a live "how to be a trainer" session.

Audience: Security managers and supervisors who train officers on post.
