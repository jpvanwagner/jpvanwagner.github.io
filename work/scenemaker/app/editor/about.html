What is Scenemaker?

Scenemaker is a desktop interactive scenario builder. Lay out scenes on a board, wire choices between them, attach portraits, audio, and documents, and export a self-contained SCORM 1.2 package that drops straight into an LMS; for branching training, dialogues, walkthroughs, or choose-your-own-path stories.

Made by

Built by Nostalginaut. Everything runs locally on your machine; your media and stories never leave your computer.

Quick tips

  • Drag from a choice's dot onto another scene to connect them.
  • Define reusable people in Characters, then drop them into a scene's cast.
  • Give a character a typing sound and extra face states for expression.
  • Set text speed and first-time-only content per line.
  • Pick a theme and tune the grid under ⚙️ Settings.
