Free asset sites that generally need no account to download. Licenses vary; most allow commercial use, some ask for attribution; so check each item's license before shipping. Tip: after importing an image, use the bg button to knock out its background.

Remember to credit your assets. Many of these ask for attribution. Keep a running list of what you use; asset, author, license, link; under Settings → Info & credits, and turn on the automatic credits screen so players see it. You can also add your own author name, website and collaborators there.

Images & photos

  • Pixabay; photos, illustrations & vectors
  • Pexels; free stock photos
  • Unsplash; high-resolution photos
  • Openclipart; public-domain clip art

Background music

  • Pixabay Music; royalty-free tracks
  • Incompetech; Kevin MacLeod's library (attribution)
  • Free Music Archive; curated free music
  • Bensound; free tracks (attribution)

Sound effects

  • Pixabay SFX; searchable sound effects
  • Mixkit; free sound effects
  • Kenney audio packs; public-domain (CC0) sounds

Game art, sprites & icons

  • Kenney; huge CC0 game-asset packs
  • OpenGameArt; community sprites, tiles & art
  • Game-icons.net; 4,000+ icons (attribution)
  • itch.io free assets; indie asset packs

Fonts

  • Google Fonts; open-source fonts
  • DaFont; free fonts (check each license)
