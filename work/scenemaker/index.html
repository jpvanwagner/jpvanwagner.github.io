What's actually in this demo

This is not a cut-down preview; it is the real editor, the same one used to build the sample story below. A couple of things that need a local backend (saving straight to disk, native SCORM export) fall back to plain browser downloads here instead, everything else works exactly as it does in the full desktop app.

🔗

Node-graph branching

Drag out scenes, wire choices between them, and see your whole story's shape at a glance. Zoom to fit, group related scenes, and route conditionally on variables, dice rolls or multi-branch logic.

🎭

Canvas screen editor

Lay out the actual game screen like a slide: drag the dialogue box, portraits and choices anywhere, stack entrance effects, and save your own reusable layout templates.

👤

Characters & variables

Portraits with swappable face states and stacked visual effects, plus a full variable system: numbers, flags or text that choices can require, change, or branch on.

🎵

Sound Studio

Record narration straight from your microphone with a live countdown, trim it on a visual waveform, and drop it onto any line.

📑

Layers

A layer is a second full stage inside the same project; link a choice to another layer with a portal to split up a big branching story without losing the overview.

📦

SCORM 1.2 export

One click builds a real SCORM 1.2 .zip with scoring and mastery options, ready to upload to any SCORM-compatible LMS. Also exports a single self-contained HTML file, or a printable PDF storyboard.
